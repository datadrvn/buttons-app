Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

buttons is made by Andrew Stokes (datadrvn). This policy covers the buttons app for iPhone.

What the app stores

The buttons you create (labels, links, colors, icons and any photos you add) are saved on your device. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect your name, contacts, location or any identifiers.

Optional web sync

If you choose to turn on “Sync with the web” and enter a set name, your buttons (including the small, compressed copies of any photos you added to them) are sent to and stored on buttons.datadrvn.com under that set name, so the same buttons appear in a web browser. Anyone who knows a set name can view and change that set, so choose a name that is hard to guess and don’t put private information in your buttons. Turning sync off stops sending data. To have a synced set deleted from the server, email us with the set name.

Photos

When you add a photo to a button, you choose it with the system photo picker; the app only receives the photo you pick, shrinks it, and stores it with that button.

Links you open

Tapping a button opens its link in Safari or the relevant app. Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Email andy.stokes@me.com with any privacy questions or deletion requests.
