QuikButtons

QuikButtons for iPhone and Android

A grid of big, tappable buttons for the links you use most. Swipe between screens, hold a button to edit or move it, and give each one a color, icon or photo.

Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests: email john@datadrvn.com.

Tips

  • Add a button: tap the dashed “+” tile.
  • Edit, move or remove: touch and hold a button.
  • Reorder: touch and hold, then drag onto another button.
  • Name a screen: tap the name at the top. Hold it, or the dots, to see and reorder all screens.
  • New screen: swipe left past the last screen and add a button there.
