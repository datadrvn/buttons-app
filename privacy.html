Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

QuikButtons is made by Andrew Stokes (datadrvn). This policy covers the QuikButtons apps for iPhone and Android.

What the app stores

The buttons you create (labels, links, colors, icons and any photos you add) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. On Android, if you have turned on Google’s device backup, Android may include them in your own backup; that backup belongs to your Google account and we can’t see it. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect your name, contacts, location or any identifiers.

Photos

When you add a photo to a button, you choose it with the system photo picker; the app only receives the photo you pick, shrinks it, and stores it with that button.

Links you open

Tapping a button opens its link in your web browser (Safari on iPhone) or the relevant app. Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Email john@datadrvn.com with any privacy questions.
